BENCH PROTOCOL

Our 28-Point Diagnostic & QA Inspection

Before any device is put on display or dispatched for delivery, it must pass every single checkpoint below:

⚡

Power & Battery Health

  • Battery cycle count & real capacity (>85% required)
  • Fast-charging circuit & USB-C Power Delivery
  • DC jack / MagSafe port physical & voltage test
  • Sleep / wake power drain measurement
🖥️

Display & Visual Subsystem

  • Full-screen RGB dead/stuck pixel check
  • Backlight uniformity & IPS glow analysis
  • Hinge resistance & ribbon cable stress test
  • Color gamut calibration & True Tone sensor test
🔥

Thermals & Stress Performance

  • 2-hour Cinebench R23 / FurMark full-load test
  • Thermal throttling curve under sustained load
  • Fan bearing noise & acoustic inspection
  • Fresh premium Arctic MX-6 / Liquid Metal repaste
💾

Storage & Memory Integrity

  • CrystalDiskInfo SSD health (0 reallocated sectors)
  • Sequential & random 4K read/write speed verification
  • Multi-pass MemTest86 RAM stability check
  • Clean OS installation with latest official drivers
⌨️

I/O, Audio & Input

  • Every keyboard key switch tested for tactile response
  • Multi-touch trackpad gesture & haptic feedback check
  • Dual stereo speaker frequency sweep (no rattle)
  • Microphone array & HD webcam clarity test
📶

Wireless & Port Connectivity

  • Wi-Fi 6 / 6E throughput & signal sensitivity
  • Bluetooth 5.x peripheral pairing & range
  • HDMI 2.1 & DisplayPort external monitor output
  • Gigabit Ethernet RJ-45 packet transfer check
